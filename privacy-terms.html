Privacy Policy

1. Information you provide

The App lets you prepare a home offer, request a home-value estimate, start a home-loan pre-qualification application, and contact us. Depending on what you choose to do, you may provide:

  • Contact details — your name, email address, and phone number, when you submit an offer or ask us to follow up.
  • Property and offer details — a property address and the offer terms you enter (price, financing, earnest money, dates, and any notes).
  • Home-value requests — a property address you enter to receive an automated estimate.
  • Loan pre-qualification details — if you start our loan application: your name, date of birth, contact information, address history, housing costs, immigration or residency status category, employment and income details, information about a co-borrower you add, and (optionally) a Social Security or ITIN number, which is handled as described in the Security section below.
  • Information from a call with our team — if you speak with us by phone, we note the answers you give (for example, the area, number of bedrooms, and price range you want, and general income and credit ranges) so we can help you. With your agreement, we use them to email you matching homes.
  • Showing requests — if you ask to see a home, the home and the days and times that work for you.
  • Messages and social media — the text messages you send us; the direct messages and comments you send to our TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook accounts; your public profile name or username on those platforms; and any phone number, email, or appointment time you share in those conversations.

Free accounts. You can use most of the App without an account. If you create a free account (you sign in with a code we email you — no password), we store your name, email, phone number, language, saved homes, saved searches, and alert settings, along with the homes you view and search while signed in. We use this to send you alerts and recommendations and so the Jason Aguirre Group agent assigned to you can help you.

2. Information stored on your device

Offers and home-value estimates you create are saved locally on your device so you can review them later. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us unless you choose to email or submit it. You can delete it at any time inside the App, or by deleting the App.

  • To prepare the draft offer or estimate you requested.
  • To respond to you and follow up about your real estate needs.
  • To contact you about home loans and real estate — using the contact information you provide, we (and the partners described below) may reach out by phone call, email, and text message, including automated text messages sent through Starling AI, the messaging platform we use, about your loan pre-qualification, home financing options, properties, and our real estate services.   • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the features you use:

  • Form delivery — when you submit an offer or request, your details are delivered to us through a form-delivery service (Web3Forms) and/or your own email app.
  • Home-value estimates — the property address you enter is sent to a third-party property-data provider (RentCast) to generate an automated estimate and nearby comparable sales.
  • Address suggestions — as you type an address, the text is sent to an address-lookup service (Photon, by Komoot, using OpenStreetMap data) to suggest matching addresses.
  • Home listings and emails — home searches use listing data from HAR through Repliers, and home and alert emails are delivered through an email-delivery service (Resend).
  • Our team — the Jason Aguirre Group agents helping you can see the information you give us and, if you have an account, the homes you view and save, so they can help you find a home.
  • The local agent assigned to your request — if you submit a form through one of our ads, we share your contact information and the answers you gave with one licensed real estate agent in your area assigned to your request, who may work with a different brokerage (for example eXp Realty, Keller Williams, RE/MAX, or Coldwell Banker), or with an agent you agree to be connected with. That agent is one of the parties you agreed could contact you, and may reach you by phone call, email, and text message, including automated or AI-assisted texts sent through Starling AI, only about the real estate services you asked about. That agent may not sell or pass your information to anyone else.
  • Messaging platform and AI — calls, texts, direct messages, and contact records are handled through Starling AI, our messaging and CRM platform, and our phone carriers. To write replies, the content of your messages is processed by artificial-intelligence models through Starling AI's service providers. These providers use your messages only to deliver and improve the service, not to market to you.
  • Social media platforms — messages and comments you send on TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook are also handled by that platform under its own privacy policy.
  • Lending and real estate partners — to give you the best service, we share the information you submit with trusted partners who help serve you: mortgage lenders and loan originators (so they can review your pre-qualification and contact you about financing), and, where relevant to your transaction, real estate service providers such as title, insurance, or credit-service partners. These partners use your information to assist with your home purchase or loan and to contact you about it.
  • Service providers and legal — we may share information with vendors who help us operate the App (for example, our form-delivery and database providers), or when required by law.

These providers process the information only to deliver their service. We encourage you to review RentCast's, Web3Forms', and Komoot's own privacy practices.
